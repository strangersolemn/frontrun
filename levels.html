THE LEVELS

ground never stops

Every FRONTRUN piece is a runner. From reveal, each earns ground — one per second, forever. Rank is ground. Burn a piece into yours and you absorb all of its ground — the burned one is gone for good. Where you sit in the field is drawn into the piece itself: the top 100 glow in their own colour, and the single runner out front wears the crown. Tap any level to see it full-screen.

Your rank is written into the art — all of it, on-chain. Whether you're the King, the Front, the Surge or the Chase, the piece renders your tier itself — everywhere it's shown, OpenSea included — and re-renders the moment the ground moves. The leaderboard just shows the whole ladder, live.
