The Race · Levels

666 runners · one crown · ground never stops

Every FRONTRUN piece is a runner. From reveal, each one earns ground — one per second, forever. Rank is ground. Burn a piece into yours and you absorb all of its ground — the burned one is gone for good. The higher you climb, the more your runner shows it: the top 100 glow in their own colour, and the single piece with the most ground wears the crown.

The crown is written into the art. Whoever holds #1 renders as the King on-chain — everywhere, even on OpenSea. The rest of the ladder lives here, on the board, updating the second the ground moves.
