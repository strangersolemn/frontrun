the race · faqhow it works
what is the race?
a game on top of frontrun. every piece is a runner. runners gain ground, and the one out front wears the crown.
what is the aim?
frontrun the rest of the pack and get crowned king.
how do i gain ground?
by holding. every piece gains ground every second, since reveal, the exact same rate for everyone. no action needed.
how do i get ahead?
burn. burn one of your pieces into another, and the survivor takes all the ground the burned piece carried. it is the only move that changes the standings.
what happens to the burned piece?
gone for good. sent to a dead address, out of the supply forever.
what does the king get?
the crown. your piece wears it, on-chain, for as long as you hold the front. one king at a time, every reign recorded forever.
what is THE FRONT?
the top ten. an elite squad at the head of the pack. the runner ahead of them all is the king.
can i flip my way to the top?
no. ground comes from holding. sell and you lose it. the race rewards the ones who stay.
is it safe? does it touch my art?
the race is a separate contract. it never changes your art. it only reads who owns what, and only moves a piece when you choose to burn it and approve it.
can it be stopped?
no. once it opens, the contract runs itself forever. no admin, no off switch, not even for the artist.
when does it start?
live in the space. the first burn opens it and the first king is crowned then and there.
do i need to do anything?
hold. that is it. when you want to climb, you burn. wallets ready for the space.
