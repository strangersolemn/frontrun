How to Burn 🔥

Burning is the only move in the game. You sacrifice one of your runners into another — the survivor absorbs all its ground and climbs toward the crown. Every burn shrinks the collection forever. Here's how, in four steps.

Read this first. Burning is permanent. The sacrificed piece is sent to a dead address and gone forever — no undo, no recovery. You need two living pieces in one wallet to burn (you keep the survivor).
1

Go to the Race & connect

Open the Race page and connect the wallet that holds your runners. Once a wallet with 2 or more living pieces connects, the burn panel appears on the page.

2

Pick sacrifice & survivor

① Sacrifice — the piece that gets destroyed. ② Survivor — the piece you keep; it absorbs every second of ground the sacrifice was carrying. Tick the box confirming you understand the sacrifice is gone forever.

tip: burn your lower pieces into the one you want to climb
3

Approve the Race — one time

First burn only: tap "approve the race · one-time" and confirm in your wallet. This lets the game move the piece you chose to burn. You only ever do this once per wallet.

4

Burn 🔥

Hit burn and confirm the transaction. The sacrifice goes to the fire, your survivor absorbs its ground and jumps up the board. If it passes the current King — the crown is yours.

Why burn? Ground rides with the piece, so a survivor that absorbs enough takes the crown. Every burn also cuts supply — fewer runners left alive, floor tightens. You burn to win, not to lose.
