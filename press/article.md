# FRONTRUN: The Race

FRONTRUN is 666 generative runners on Ethereum. The art lives fully on-chain. The Race is a game that runs on top of it — a burn-only contest for the crown, or a spot in The Front, played out in the art itself.

## How it works

Every piece is a runner. From reveal, each one earns **ground** — one point per second, forever, at the same rate for all 666. Hold a piece and it keeps accruing.

**Rank is ground.** The piece with the most ground is out front, and that runner wears the **crown** — the King. There is one King at a time. When another piece passes it, the crown moves.

There is one move in the game: **burn**. Burn one of your pieces into another, and the survivor absorbs all of the sacrifice's ground. The burned piece is gone for good. That is the whole game — hold and accrue slowly, or sacrifice pieces to push one runner up the field.

Where you sit is drawn into the piece. The field is split into tiers:

- **King** — rank 1. Wears the crown.
- **Front** — ranks 2–10.
- **Surge** — ranks 11–25.
- **Chase** — ranks 26–100.
- **Field** — everyone else.

Your tier is rendered into the art on-chain, and it re-renders as the ground moves. Climb, and the piece changes — everywhere it is shown, OpenSea included.

## What you get

Every FRONTRUN piece now renders its live rank on-chain. As a holder you get:

- A **runner** that accrues ground every second from reveal.
- A **place on the board** — a live leaderboard ranking all 666 by ground.
- A way to **climb** — burn pieces to bank their ground into one survivor.
- **Tier art** that updates itself as your rank changes, on-chain, with no server and nothing to flip.
- The **crown** if you take the front — worn by your piece, on-chain, for as long as you hold it, with every reign recorded.

The Race is a separate, burn-only contract. It reads ownership and moves the pieces you approve. It does nothing else to the collection.

## Why

I wanted a collection that keeps moving after mint. Most go quiet once the art is set. This one has a live board, a crown that can be taken, and a record of every reign — a small on-chain sport that runs as long as people play it.

Ground never stops. The field is even now, everyone tied at the line. The first burn opens the game and crowns the first King.

## Starting

The Race goes live with the first burn, in the Space. To play: approve the Race contract, then burn one piece into another. The survivor takes the ground; the front takes the crown.

666 runners. One crown.
