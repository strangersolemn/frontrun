# FRONTRUN Space — Co-Host Brief

**You're co-hosting with me tonight. Here's the 60-second version + questions you can throw me so the story tells itself.**

## What it is (so you're grounded)
- **FRONTRUN** = 666 generative runners on Ethereum. Art lives fully on-chain.
- **The Race** = a game on top of the collection. It went live today.
- Every piece earns **ground**: 1 point per second, from reveal, same for all 666.
- **Rank = ground.** Most ground is out front and wears the **crown** (the King). One at a time.
- One move: **burn.** Burn one of your pieces into another, the survivor absorbs all its ground. The burned one is gone forever.
- Your rank is **drawn into the art** and re-renders as you climb (King, Front, Surge, Chase, Field) — everywhere, OpenSea included.
- After the final burn, I **renounce** — the contract locks on-chain forever.

## Tonight's flow
1. I open, quick word on the last few days, then into the game.
2. How it works (you help me draw it out — questions below).
3. The levels / tiers.
4. Fairness + open floor.
5. The renounce + the vision.
6. Q&A from the room.

9. "You said you're renouncing. What does that mean and why does it matter?"
10. "What's this 'front run the culture' thing about?"
11. "For someone who wants in right now — what do they do?"

## Curveballs (hand them to me; here's roughly how I'll answer so you can steer)
- **"Is this a rug?"** -> Nobody bought for the burn; it was announced after mint. And I'm renouncing — a rug keeps the keys.

- **"Why destroy art?"** -> It's the game. Stakes and scarcity. Playing is optional; the art's alive either way.
- **"When does it end?"** -> It doesn't. Ground never stops, the crown's always contestable.
