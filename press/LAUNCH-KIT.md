## APOLOGY — GC ONLY (group chat, the full one — the apology lives here, not the TL)

I owe you the truth about the last five days, so here it is.

I messed up when I said it'd be done "tomorrow." I gave myself a deadline that was impossible to hit, and I didn't realise how much was actually left. It turned into iteration after iteration — four different versions of the art before I was happy. I wasn't going to ship something I didn't stand behind.

I've been on this around the clock, every spare minute, every day, always about 5% from deploying — and that last 5% kept moving. Sepolia testing was its own nightmare; I couldn't get enough testnet ETH to run it properly, even across multiple wallets. A tragic personal event landed in the middle of all this too. I'm not going to get into it, and I'm not using it as an excuse — but I won't pretend it didn't take something out of me.

The final deploy was 14 transactions. Changing the actual art file on-chain is a delicate operation — a far bigger job than I thought going in. The weekend came, and late Sunday night I deployed the final piece.

I went quiet because I had nothing to hand you yet, and I'd already said I would. I wasn't going to come back with more "soon." I've been on the edge of writing this for four days.

Where it stands: some people left, some sold, the price came down a bit. Some new people came in. The floor is a small thing to bring back, and I'll handle it.  ← [your call: promise people will hold you to]

If a few quiet days shook you out, that's your call — but understand what this is. Do you think I'd walk away from five years of work over nothing? Nobody bought this drop for the burn — the burn was announced after the mint. It was never that.  ← [your call: the "shook you out" jab — GC only, not on TL]

Once the final burn goes live, I'm renouncing. After that the contract is locked on-chain, forever — no one can touch it, me included.

I won't promise I'll always be in here. When I'm deep in creative mode I go heads-down. But I'm never leaving. This is where I live. This is part of my legacy.

To everyone who stuck around — thank you. Genuinely.
