01 / THE WORK

Every wallet, a different piece.

The art is built from your wallet. Its address isn't a label on the work — it's the material the work is made of.

01 — at mint

Traits & palette

02 — the holder

The look

The numbers in the holding address map the motion, the tunnel, the form. In some, the address itself becomes the tunnel.

●Palette — the piece's colours, stamped by an immutable seed the second it's mintedlocked forever
●Figure treatment — how the runner is painted: static, scanlines, stripes, datamoshlocked forever
○Tunnel shape — hex, square, triangle, twisted… drawn from the piece's seed and the holding wallet togetherfollows the holder
○Wall texture — noise, geometry, or text. When it's text, the holder's address literally fills the wallsfollows the holder
○Motion & density — speed and weight of the tunnel, mapped from the holder's digitsfollows the holder

So when a piece changes hands, it stays itself — same runner, same colours — and repaints its world for whoever holds it. New tunnel, new digits, new motion. Every transfer is the same piece running a new lane. And each piece reads a wallet its own way: hold two and your address paints two different worlds.

02 / PERMANENCE

Fully on-chain. All of it.

Most "on-chain" art keeps its image on a server. FRONTRUN doesn't. The art, the data, and the picture all live on Ethereum itself — nothing hosted, nothing to rot. Permanence isn't a promise here. It's a property of how it's built.
